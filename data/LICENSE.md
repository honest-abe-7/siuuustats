Club data (M3) is adapted from Spanish Wikipedia's "Anexo:Partidos de Cristiano Ronaldo con clubes" and the English Wikipedia club season articles and career table (all CC BY-SA 4.0, Wikipedia contributors), and from the Kaggle dataset "Ronaldo CR7 All Goals GOAT" by sigmaborov (CC BY-SA 4.0, https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sigmaborov/ronaldo-cr7-all-goals-goat). Three match pages (zerozero.pt, UEFA.com, Athletic Club) are cited for single date checks only. Nothing was copied from them.
