# Big Games & Clutch

> **Argument page.** Numbers marked **[ledger]** are computed by `scripts/lib/stats.mjs` from the SIUUU Stats match-by-match ledger. That now covers every club and international match, and club matches carry their stage (final, semi-final and so on). **International matches do not have a stage in the ledger yet.** Any claim about a Portugal knockout round therefore cites UEFA, BBC or Wikipedia, and is labelled *(stage: cited, not ledger)*.

The test of greatness is not how many you score, but **when**. Here is the record in finals, in knockout ties and in matches decided by a single goal.

---

## 1. Champions League finals

| Final | Result | Ronaldo |
|---|---|---|
| 2008, Manchester United v Chelsea | 1–1, United won on penalties | **Scored United's only goal**[^wiki] **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals] |
| 2009, Manchester United v Barcelona | 0–2 | No goal **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals] |
| 2014, Real Madrid v Atlético | 4–1 after extra time | **Scored**: a 120th-minute penalty[^uefa-honour] **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals] |
| 2016, Real Madrid v Atlético | 1–1, won on penalties | **Scored the winning shoot-out kick**[^wiki] (shoot-out kicks are not goals in the ledger) |
| 2017, Real Madrid v Juventus | 4–1 | **Scored twice**[^wiki] **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals] |
| 2018, Real Madrid v Liverpool | 3–1 | No goal. Won his fifth title, the first player to win five[^wiki] |

- He is the **only player to score in three Champions League finals**: 4 goals in 6 finals. **[ledger]**[^ledger-ucl] [^uefa-records]
- He has won **five Champions Leagues** (2008, 2014, 2016, 2017 and 2018).[^uefa-honour]

## 2. Champions League knockout rounds: the record book

Source: ledger, `stageClass()` over `matches.stage`.[^ledger-ucl] **67 knockout goals is the competition record.**[^uefa-records] He has scored **140 Champions League goals in 183 matches** (main competition). Counting the 2005 qualifier at Debrecen, it is 141 in 187. **[ledger]**[^ledger-ucl] [^uefa-honour]

- He is the first player to score **hat-tricks in consecutive Champions League knockout matches**.[^wa-records] That was 2016–17: a hat-trick against Bayern in the quarter-final second leg, then another against Atlético in the semi-final first leg.[^uefa-ucl100] [^uefa-atm17]
- **2016 quarter-final, Wolfsburg:** Madrid lost the first leg 2–0. He scored a **hat-trick in the 3–0 return** to send them through.[^uefa-wob16]
- **2017 quarter-final, Bayern:** He scored both goals in the 2–1 first-leg win, becoming the first player to reach 100 goals in UEFA club competition.[^wiki] In the return leg his hat-trick took him to **100 Champions League goals**.[^uefa-ucl100]
- **2018 quarter-final, Juventus:** He scored twice in a 3–0 away win. The second was the bicycle kick that earned a standing ovation from the home fans.[^wiki]
- **2019 round of 16, Atlético:** Juventus were 2–0 down from the first leg. He scored a **hat-trick in the 3–0 second leg**.[^wiki]

## 3. Club finals and trophy-deciders

Across all competitions he has scored **24 goals in 37 club finals** (super cups count as finals) and scored in **19 of them**. **[ledger]**[^ledger-big] The highlights:

- **2004 FA Cup final:** scored the opening goal of United's 3–0 win over Millwall.[^wiki] **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals]
- **2006 League Cup final:** scored United's third goal in the 4–0 win over Wigan.[^wiki]
- **2011 Copa del Rey final:** scored the **winner against Barcelona**, a header in extra time.[^uefa-copa11] **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals]
- **2014 UEFA Super Cup:** scored **both goals** in the 2–0 win over Sevilla. **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals]
- **2016 Club World Cup final:** scored a **hat-trick** as Madrid beat Kashima Antlers 4–2 after extra time: a 60th-minute penalty to make it 2–2, then two in extra time.[^fifa-cwc16] **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals]
- **2017 Club World Cup final:** scored the only goal against Grêmio. He was the first player to score in consecutive Club World Cup finals, and holds the competition's scoring record with 7 goals.[^wa-records] **[ledger]**[^ledger-comp]
- **2018 Supercoppa Italiana (played 16 January 2019):** scored the only goal against AC Milan, a header.[^wiki] **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals]
- **2023 Arab Club Champions Cup final:** scored **both goals** (74' and 98') in Al-Nassr's 2–1 extra-time win over Al-Hilal.[^wiki] **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals] This competition is not FIFA-recognised; see the Myths page.
- **Saudi Pro League 2025–26, final day:** scored **twice in a 4–1 win over Damac**, a 63rd-minute free kick and a goal in the 81st minute, to clinch the title.[^bein-title] [^espn-title] **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals]

## 4. Portugal in tournaments

- **Euro 2004 semi-final:** opened the scoring with a header in the 2–1 win over the Netherlands.[^bbc-euro2004] *(stage: cited, not ledger)*
- **Euro 2012 quarter-final:** scored the **79th-minute winner** against the Czech Republic, a header.[^uefa-cze12] He finished as the tournament's joint top scorer.[^wiki] *(stage: cited, not ledger)*
- **Euro 2016 semi-final:** scored a header in the 2–0 win over Wales, and his shot was turned in by Nani for the second. Portugal went on to win their **first major trophy**.[^uefa-wal16] [^wiki] *(stage: cited, not ledger)*
- **Euro 2020:** won the **Golden Boot**.[^wiki]
- **Most goals at European Championship finals tournaments: 14.** **[ledger]**[^ledger-records] [^wa-records]
- **2014 World Cup play-off against Sweden:** scored **all four of Portugal's goals** over the two legs.[^wiki] That includes a hat-trick in Stockholm (50', 77', 79'), the last of which made it 3–2.[^uefa-swe13] **[ledger]**[^ledger-goals]
- **2018 World Cup:** a hat-trick against Spain in a 3–3 draw, finished with an **88th-minute equaliser**. That made him the oldest player to score a World Cup hat-trick.[^wiki] [^ledger-goals] He then scored the only goal against Morocco.[^wiki]
- **2019 Nations League semi-final:** **hat-trick** against Switzerland (25', 88', 90'). Portugal won the tournament.[^uefa-sui19] [^wiki-list] *(stage: cited, not ledger)*
- **2025 Nations League Finals:** scored the **semi-final winner against Germany** and the **equaliser in the final against Spain** (2–2). Portugal won 5–3 on penalties.[^uefa-unl25-sf] [^uefa-unl25-f] He was top scorer at both the 2019 and 2025 Finals (shared in 2025).[^wiki] *(stage: cited, not ledger)*
- **2026 World Cup:** became the **first player to score at six World Cups**, with two goals against Uzbekistan.[^reuters-6wc] [^ap-6wc]

## 5. Match-winners, equalisers and late goals **[ledger]**

These use `winningGoals()` and `equalisers()` in `scripts/lib/stats.mjs`, which have unit tests. A **winning goal** is a goal in a match his team won on the score (not a shoot-out, and not an awarded result) that took his team to one more than the opponent's final total. An **equaliser** is a goal after which the score was level.[^ledger-career]

| Measure | Career | Clubs | Portugal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Goals | 981 | 835 | 146 |
| **Winning goals** | **322** | 270 | 52 |
| …of which in one-goal wins | 107 | 85 | 22 |
| …of which penalties | 63 | 55 | 8 |
| **Equalisers** | **99** | 81 | 18 |
| Goals in the 80th minute or later | 180 of 959 with a minute | 155 of 835 | 25 of 124 |

Source: ledger.[^ledger-career] [^ledger-goals] One third of his career goals (322 of 981) were the goal that won the match.

Winning goals for Portugal at major tournaments and Nations League Finals, straight from the ledger:[^ledger-goals]

- Euro 2008 v Czech Republic (63', made it 2–1 in a 3–1 win)
- Euro 2012 v Netherlands (74', 2–1) and v Czech Republic (79', 1–0; quarter-final *(stage: cited, not ledger)*)
- 2014 World Cup v Ghana (80', 2–1)
- Euro 2016 v Wales (50'; semi-final *(stage: cited, not ledger)*)
- 2018 World Cup v Morocco (4', 1–0)
- 2019 Nations League Finals v Switzerland (88')
- 2025 Nations League Finals v Germany (68')
- 2026 World Cup v Uzbekistan (6', 5–0)

One example of late goals: on 1 September 2021 against the Republic of Ireland, Portugal were 1–0 down. He scored in the 89th and 90th minutes (as recorded in the ledger) to turn the match into a 2–1 win. Those were his 110th and 111th international goals.[^ledger-goals] [^wiki-list]

## Fair points

- **World Cup knockout matches** are his weak spot. His only World Cup knockout goal is a **penalty against Croatia in the 2026 round of 32**. Portugal went out to Spain in the next round, and commentators rated his 2026 tournament as low-impact.[^wiki] *(stage: cited, not ledger)*
- In the **Euro 2016 final** he went off injured after 25 minutes. Portugal won without him on the pitch.[^wiki]
- He **did not score at Euro 2024**. **[ledger]**[^ledger-comp]
- He did not score in **18 of his 37 club finals**, including the 2009 and 2018 Champions League finals. **[ledger]**[^ledger-big]

The case does not need these hidden. Across five Champions League titles, three finals with a goal and 67 knockout goals, the big-game record holds up.
