## Source issues noticed while fact-checking (for the data team)

- **ESPN (21 May 2026)** says Ronaldo has won "domestic league titles in Portugal, England, Spain, Italy and Saudi Arabia". Wikipedia's honours list shows no Primeira Liga title, only the 2002 Supertaça with Sporting. The content pages do **not** use the ESPN claim.
- **Wikipedia "Cristiano Ronaldo" (rev 1381391040)** says Ronaldo "broke" the men's international scoring record with his two goals against France on 23 Jun 2021. The ledger has those as goals 108 and 109, and goals 110 and 111 came against Ireland on 1 Sep 2021. The content pages avoid the claim. Not reported upstream: no account, no edits.
- **Wikipedia lead says 141 international wins.** The ledger has 142, which is already a known difference (`wikipedia-lead-intl-wins`). The pages use 142.
- **Wikipedia's career-achievements records table** says 979 career goals, while the main article says 981 (club 835 + international 146, as of 20 Oct 2026). The pages use 981.
