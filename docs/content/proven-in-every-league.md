# Proven in Every League

> **Argument page.** Numbers marked **[ledger]** are computed by `scripts/lib/stats.mjs` from the SIUUU Stats match-by-match ledger, which now covers every club match (to 20 October 2026) as well as every Portugal cap. Each club season and competition reconciles exactly with Wikipedia's career-statistics table (revision 1381391040). Awards and titles are cited from Wikipedia, official sites and the press, because the trophies and awards tables are still empty.

Some players dominate one league. Ronaldo has moved country four times and finished as a league's top scorer in **four different top divisions**, the first player ever to do so.[^wiki] [^goal-4leagues]

## England: Manchester United

- **103 Premier League goals** in 236 appearances over two spells. **[ledger]**[^ledger-season]
- **2007–08:** won the **Premier League Golden Boot with 31 goals** (in 34 league games)[^pl-0708] [^wiki] **[ledger]**[^ledger-season], the European Golden Shoe (the first winger to win it), the Champions League, and his **first Ballon d'Or**, at 23.[^wiki]
- **Titles:** three Premier Leagues in a row (2006–07, 2007–08 and 2008–09), the 2004 FA Cup, two League Cups, the 2008 Champions League and the 2008 Club World Cup.[^wiki]
- He won the PFA Players' Player of the Year award in **2007 and 2008**, and was the first Premier League player named FIFA World Player of the Year.[^wiki]
- He came back at 36 and scored **24 goals in all competitions in 2021–22** (18 in the league) **[ledger]**[^ledger-season], earning a place in the Premier League Team of the Year.[^wiki]

## Spain: Real Madrid

- **311 La Liga goals in 292 appearances**, more than a goal a game. **[ledger]**[^ledger-season]
- **Real Madrid's all-time top scorer: 450 goals in 438 matches.** **[ledger]**[^ledger-club] [^wiki] The club's own site credits him with 451.[^rm-official] The ledger keeps 450, which matches Wikipedia. The gap is now documented in the repo's `known-differences.json` and `METHODOLOGY.md`.[^ledger-rm451] At Real Sociedad on 18 Sep 2010 (1-2), Ronaldo's 74th-minute free kick went in off Pepe's back. The official record, ESPN and Wikipedia credit Pepe.[^espn-rsoc10] [^wiki] Real Madrid and Marca credit Ronaldo, and so did Pepe himself, as Sky Sports reported.[^sky-rsoc10] [^rm-official]
- **Pichichi Trophy** (La Liga top scorer) in 2010–11, 2013–14 and 2014–15. **European Golden Shoe** in 2010–11, 2013–14 (shared) and 2014–15, which made him the first player to win the Golden Shoe in different leagues.[^wiki] [^wa-records]
- He was the **first player to score 40 goals in a La Liga season** (2010–11), and scored **48 league goals in 2014–15**.[^wiki] **[ledger]**[^ledger-season]
- He is the only player with **30 or more league goals in six consecutive La Liga seasons**, and the fastest to 300 La Liga goals (286 matches).[^wa-records]
- **Titles:** La Liga 2011–12 (the 100-point season) and 2016–17, two Copas del Rey, four Champions Leagues and three Club World Cups.[^wiki]

## Italy: Juventus

- **81 Serie A goals in 98 appearances**, and **101 goals in 134 matches** for Juventus in all competitions. **[ledger]**[^ledger-club] [^ledger-season]
- **Capocannoniere 2020–21** (Serie A top scorer) with **29 goals** in 33 games **[ledger]**[^ledger-season]. That made him the first player to top the scoring charts in England, Spain and Italy.[^uefa-capo21] [^espn-3leagues]
- **Titles:** Serie A 2018–19 and 2019–20, the 2021 Coppa Italia and two Supercoppe Italiane. Winning the 2019 title made him the **first player to win the league in England, Spain and Italy**. The 2021 Coppa Italia made him the first to win every major domestic trophy in all three countries.[^wiki]
- He was **Serie A Most Valuable Player in 2019** and Serie A Footballer of the Year in 2019 and 2020. He was the first player to reach 50 goals in each of the Premier League, La Liga and Serie A, and set the Juventus record for goals in a season (37, in 2019–20).[^wiki] **[ledger]**[^ledger-season]

## Saudi Arabia: Al-Nassr

- **106 Saudi Pro League goals in 115 appearances**, and **134 goals in 158 matches** for Al-Nassr in all competitions (to 20 October 2026). **[ledger]**[^ledger-club] [^ledger-season]
- **Single-season league record: 35 goals in 31 games in 2023–24** **[ledger]**[^ledger-season], beating Abderrazak Hamdallah's 34.[^bbc-spl35] [^reuters-spl35]
- **Golden Boot in 2023–24 and 2024–25.**[^wiki]
- **League champion in 2025–26.** He scored twice in the final-day 4–1 win over Damac as Al-Nassr won the title by two points. It was the club's first league title since 2018–19.[^bein-title] [^espn-title]
- On 23 August 2025 his 100th Al-Nassr goal made him the first player to score **100 goals for four different clubs**.[^ap-100x4]
- **Honest context:** his 28 league goals in 2025–26 **[ledger]**[^ledger-season] put him third in the scoring chart, behind Julián Quiñones (33) and Ivan Toney (32).[^bein-title] The 2023 Arab Club Champions Cup (6 goals, and he scored both goals in the final) counts in the totals, as it does on Wikipedia. FIFA does not recognise the competition, though, so the ledger flags it: 829 club goals and 975 career goals without it. **[ledger]**[^ledger-club] [^espn-title]

## Portugal: where it started, and the national team

- **Sporting CP:** he made his first-team debut at 17 on 14 August 2002. On 7 October 2002 he scored his first two goals, against Moreirense. He won the 2002 Supertaça.[^wiki] For Sporting he scored 5 goals in 31 matches: 3 in the league and 2 in the Taça de Portugal. **[ledger]**[^ledger-club] [^ledger-season]
- In 2025 **Liga Portugal named him "The Best of All Time"**.[^ligaportugal] [^espn-ligapt]
- **For Portugal: 146 goals in 234 caps.** Both are men's world records. **[ledger]**[^ledger-summary] [^wiki]
  - Portugal's record with him on the pitch: **W142 D54 L38** (shoot-outs counted as draws). **[ledger]**[^ledger-summary]
  - **14 goals at European Championship finals** (a record) and **41 in World Cup qualifying** (a record). **[ledger]**[^ledger-records] [^wa-records]
  - **Trophies:** Euro 2016, and the Nations League in 2019 and 2025.[^wiki]

## And across borders: the Champions League

The one competition that pits all these leagues against each other:

- **140 goals in 183 appearances** (main competition), the all-time record, across three clubs in three countries. **[ledger]**[^ledger-ucl] Counting the 2005 qualifier at Debrecen, it is 141 in 187. UEFA's own published figure is 140 in 183. He was the competition's **top scorer seven times**, including 2007–08 with Manchester United and six seasons in a row with Real Madrid.[^uefa-honour]
